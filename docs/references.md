Each reference was checked against its source on 2026-10-20. Nothing here is quoted beyond what the
source says.

## Not claimed

This repository is a demonstration of method on a public 2017 model. It does not reproduce, and
makes no statement about, the lab's current models, the ReSCUED twin, or real patient behaviour.
